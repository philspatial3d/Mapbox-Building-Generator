# Landing Concepts - Design Thesis Summary

## 01. Urban Operations Atlas (`landing/landing-atlas.html`)
- Thesis: planning-instrument aesthetic with map-legibility hierarchy.
- Intentional interaction: D3 process graph with animated directional links and stage-level hover explanations.

## 02. Structural Pulse (`landing/landing-structural.html`)
- Thesis: structural expression and operational confidence.
- Intentional interaction: Three.js live wireframe tower with pointer-reactive camera influence and FPS status meter.

## 03. Civic Fabric Studio (`landing/landing-civic-fabric.html`)
- Thesis: city-grain-first concept framing.
- Intentional interaction: p5 parcel-field simulation that responds to cursor position and highlights corridor pressure zones.
- Refined direction: full vertical product narrative with stronger architectural drafting language and screenshot handoff placeholder.

## 04. Program Control Desk (`landing/landing-control-desk.html`)
- Thesis: measurable tradeoff dashboard for typology-fit decisions.
- Intentional interaction: D3 matrix heatmap with hover-driven fit/rationale detail output.
- Refined direction: harder linework, denser professional hierarchy, and explicit architect-facing value proposition.

## 05. Material Ledger (`landing/landing-material-ledger.html`)
- Thesis: sustainability expressed as explicit, auditable design decisions.
- Intentional interaction: Three.js shell preview plus D3 score ring with segment-level metric narratives.
