Structural Pulse

Design thesis: Present the product as an engineered instrument where structure is visible and legible. The animated wireframe tower acts as a persistent metaphor for controllable systems: envelope, core, circulation, and facade are seen as one stack.

exoskeleton-aware core-first planning floorplan mode parcel-constrained generation

The generator works by taking a selected map footprint, resolving massing constraints and structural layers, then producing interior logic (stairs, corridors, room programs) by typology. You can inspect both tower form and 2D typical-floor plan from the same source geometry.

ParcelClick an existing extruded context building or upload GeoJSON.
ParametersTune floors, taper, facade, and structural systems.
GenerationCompute shell, core, windows, stairs, and room programs.
DeliverySwitch to floorplan mode or export GeoJSON for GIS/BIM workflows.
