Civic Fabric Studio is the architect-focused front door to Mapbox Building Generator. It turns context geometry into buildable envelope options with floorplan logic, so teams can move from "what if" to a defensible massing direction quickly and with less iteration waste.

What the product does

Select a parcel or upload a footprint, adjust envelope and facade controls, and generate coherent stairs, core, and room programs. Toggle floorplan mode to inspect a typical floor at zero pitch and export your geometry to downstream tools.

For architects

Rapidly test massing and circulation options before committing to detailed BIM work.

For planning + GIS

Keep parcel reality in the loop and export machine-readable outputs for analysis and mapping.

For iterative design reviews

Compare options quickly in the same coordinate context and preserve assumptions in a repeatable workflow.

How the generator works

A deterministic pipeline converts map context into architectural geometry. Decorative motion is secondary; the core interaction is predictable control over form, structure, and program.

Step 01
Capture footprint and context Click an existing extruded building or upload Polygon/MultiPolygon GeoJSON. The footprint is cleaned, validated, and prepared for generation.
Step 02
Set envelope and facade intent Tune floors, taper mode, podium, facade opacity, mullions, exoskeleton, and roof systems using direct controls.
Step 03
Generate interior logic Build stairs, stairwells, floor slabs, core, corridors, and typology-aware room partitions from the same geometry source.
Step 04
Inspect and deliver Switch to floorplan view for a typical floor at zero pitch, then export GeoJSON to handoff workflows or the Parametric QGIS plugin.
