Material Ledger

Design thesis: Sustainability should look measurable, not abstract. This concept pairs a calm material-forward palette with a live score ring and structural shell preview so users can reason about design intent and performance at the same time.

The generator pipeline starts with footprint capture, applies parametric envelope and facade controls, then builds floorplate logic and exports geometry for GIS. The landing page emphasizes accountability: every visual treatment maps to a building decision.

Footprint FidelityMap parcel selection and uploaded GeoJSON are normalized before generation.
Structural StackCore, slabs, exoskeleton, and facade are generated from one parameter set.
Program LogicFloorplans align to typology: residential, office, warehouse, data center.
GIS DeliveryExport to GeoJSON and continue in the Parametric QGIS plugin.
