Why teams adopt it

The interface is intentionally strict: hard linework, explicit controls, deterministic geometry, and an output path that stays usable after the design meeting. The payoff is faster option generation with fewer ambiguous handoffs between concept design, internal review, asset production, and GIS analysis.

How the generator works

A deterministic workflow designed for architectural teams that need speed without losing control.

Stage 01
Select or upload a footprint Click an existing building parcel or upload Polygon or MultiPolygon GeoJSON from your site package.
Stage 02
Set massing and facade systems Tune tower core geometry, taper behavior, envelope opacity, mullions, podium logic, and roof profile.
Stage 03
Generate interior structure Build stairs, slabs, service core, corridors, room bands, and floorplan partitions from one control panel.
Stage 04
Inspect and export Use floorplan view for plan-level review, then export geometry to downstream GIS and design analysis workflows.
